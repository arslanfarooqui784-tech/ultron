WHAT ANDROID ALLOWS

✅ SIM calls: caller name and number, answered / missed / declined, repeated calls, declining unwanted callers, texting missed callers by SMS.
✅ WhatsApp, Instagram, Snapchat, Telegram, Messenger calls: incoming and missed calls are seen and logged, and you are told about them.
❌ ULTRON cannot answer a call and talk to the caller with its AI voice. Android does not let any normal app speak into a phone call or hear the caller, and WhatsApp, Instagram, Snapchat and Telegram have no way for another app to answer their calls. Only the phone company's network (call forwarding to an AI receptionist number) can do that.
❌ Call recording and live call transcripts: not possible for the same reason, so ULTRON only reports call details (who, when, how often), never what was said on a call.
